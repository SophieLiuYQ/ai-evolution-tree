## What it is

Evo 2 is a 40-billion-parameter genomic foundation model trained on 9.3 trillion nucleotides from 100,000+ species (the OpenGenome 2 dataset). 1-million-token context window. Open weights under Apache 2.0. Developed by Arc Institute with NVIDIA, Stanford, UC Berkeley, and UCSF.

## Why it's a foundation-class model

- **Cross-domain.** Trained on DNA from all domains of life — bacteria, archaea, eukaryotes. The same model handles human variants and bacterial genome design.
- **Functional prediction without disease labels.** ~90% accuracy predicting whether unseen BRCA1 mutations are pathogenic, learned purely from cross-species sequence patterns.
- **Generative.** Designs novel genomes up to bacterial scale (~1M nucleotides), not just classifies existing ones.

## Architecture note

Uses StripedHyena 2 — a hybrid of convolutional and attention layers designed for very long sequences. A pure Transformer at 1M-token context would be infeasible at training scale; the hybrid is part of why this scale was reachable.

## Where it sits

Biology's foundation-model moment. AlphaFold 2/3 solved structure prediction; Evo 2 makes the analogous claim for sequence understanding and design. Fully open, including training data — a deliberate posture against closed biotech platforms.
