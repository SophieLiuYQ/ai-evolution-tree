## What it is

Marble is the first generally-available model from World Labs (Fei-Fei Li, Justin Johnson, Christoph Lassner, Ben Mildenhall). It generates editable 3D environments from text or image inputs, producing assets that drop into game engines and VFX pipelines.

## Why this isn't another video model

The output is 3D scene representation — geometry, materials, lighting — not video frames. That makes it structurally different from Sora/Veo/Kling. A 3D scene can be relit, re-cameraed, re-edited downstream; a video can't. This is the bet underlying World Labs' "spatial intelligence" framing: that the next AI plateau requires models that understand and produce 3D space natively.

## Market signal

The $1B Series B in February 2026, with AMD, Autodesk, NVIDIA, and Fidelity participating, is a notable coalition: chip vendors, creative-tools vendors, and finance simultaneously betting on spatial intelligence as foundational rather than a niche.

## Where it sits

Three world-model branches crystallized in 2025–2026:
- **Embodied / robotics** (NVIDIA Cosmos, Genie 3) — physical AI policy training
- **Creative 3D** (Marble) — editable scene assets for media
- **Entertainment video** (Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway) — finished video output

Marble is the leading example of the second branch.
