## What it is

Cosmos-Predict 2.5 is NVIDIA's world foundation model for physical AI — a video-generation system targeted at robotics, autonomous driving, and simulator pipelines rather than consumer content. The 2.5 release unifies Text2World, Image2World, and Video2World generation in a single flow-based diffusion model, with vision-language grounding from Cosmos-Reason1.

Trained on 200 million curated video clips, refined with RL post-training. Released open-weights at 2B and 14B scales.

## Why it's a foundation-class model

- **Action-conditioned variants** (Feb 2026) let it serve as a learned world model inside reinforcement-learning loops for robot policies — concrete benchmarks on RoboCasa and Libero.
- **Cosmos-Transfer 2.5** (Dec 2025) handles Sim2Real and Real2Real translation; **Cosmos-Reason 2** (Feb 2026) provides the language interface. Together the three form a stack rather than a single model.
- **Open weights with a permissive license**, downloaded 2M+ times by early 2026.

## Where it sits

The "world model" category split into two branches in 2025–2026: entertainment-grade video (Sora, Veo, Kling) and physical-AI grade simulation (Cosmos, Genie 3, World Labs Marble). Cosmos is the open option in the second branch.
