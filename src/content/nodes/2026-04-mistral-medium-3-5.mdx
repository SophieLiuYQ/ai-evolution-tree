## What it is

Mistral Medium 3.5 is a 128-billion-parameter dense Transformer, released April 29, 2026 under Apache 2.0 with a 256K context window. It unifies what were three separate Mistral models — Medium 3.1 for general use, Magistral for reasoning, Devstral 2 for coding — into one weight set with a mode toggle.

## Why this one is notable

- **77.6% SWE-Bench Verified.** Within ~2 points of Claude Sonnet 4.6 (the closed-source coding leader), at meaningfully lower per-token cost.
- **Dense, not MoE.** Almost every other 2026 frontier release is sparse Mixture-of-Experts. Mistral's bet is that dense models fine-tune cleanly and serve more predictably for mixed enterprise workloads.
- **Apache 2.0 with an agent.** Shipped alongside Vibe, Mistral's remote-agent CLI that opens PRs against your repos. Open weights *with* an agent harness is rare.

## Where it fits

This is the model an enterprise picks when API egress isn't allowed but coding-agent capability is needed. Closed models are still ahead on raw benchmarks; Medium 3.5 is ahead on what's actually deployable in regulated environments.
